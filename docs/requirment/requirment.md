**Project Guidelines**

Pick a quantitative / coding research paper you find interesting (model, algorithm, simulation,
or analysis). Before starting, discuss the paper and scope with me for approval.

**Projects**
can be done solo or in pairs (max 2 students).
Part 1 — Reproduce: Implement the core method and reproduce key results of the paper
(e.g., a main figure/metric).
Part 2 — Extend (proof of concept): Propose 2–4 follow-up ideas, then choose one and test
it with a small, well-defined experiment: state a hypothesis, define metrics, run minimal code,
and produce concrete results (plots/tables/failure cases).
Deliverables: short report (6 pages, template will be shared) + runnable code.

Presentation: I reserve the possibility to meet (in person or zoom) to go over your project for
30min. This is also to make sure that it was not performed by AI. The use of AI is allowed as
long as you conceptually understand and own all the steps and code of your project.
